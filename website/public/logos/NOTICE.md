# AI platform marks

These marks come from Lobe Icons (https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), published
under the MIT License by LobeHub. Each mark is a trademark of its owner and is shown
only to indicate that StashDock can hand context to that AI app.

`kimi-app.svg` places the unchanged Kimi mark (`kimi-color.svg`) on a black rounded
square, as the Kimi app icon shows it. `meta-color.svg` stands for Meta's AI apps.
